ABOUT STEELLET

Built to outlast
the device era.

Steellet began with a narrow question: how can the root of a digital wallet survive fire, water, software failure, and the disappearance of a vendor? The answer became a mechanical object made to be read, verified, and kept.

01

Physical first

The recovery material is steel. No battery, firmware, cloud account, or proprietary reader is required.

02

Open recovery

The product preserves standard BIP39 entropy, so recovery does not depend on Steellet remaining in business.

03

Verify everything

The encoding is visible and can be checked layer by layer before the vault is closed.
