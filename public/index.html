07 / ESSENTIALS

Four useful answers.

Is Steellet an electronic hardware wallet?

No. It is a physical backup for wallet entropy and does not sign transactions.

Does the offline tool upload my seed?

No. Wait until it reports “cached,” disconnect every network, and only then use it with real recovery material.

Does the current object support 12 words?

The tool converts 12-word phrases, but the production physical map is currently defined for 24-word, 256-bit entropy.

Can the BIP39 seed be converted back to words?

No. The reversible value is entropy. The later 512-bit BIP39 seed cannot be reversed into the mnemonic.
